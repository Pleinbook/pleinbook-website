## The brush library

<SearchTerms terms="brushes, library, choose brush, presets, groups, search brushes, new brush, duplicate brush, icon" />

Tap the brush at the top of the tool rail to open the library. Tap a brush to paint with it.

<Screenshot src={BrushesLibrary} alt="The brush library: the kit along the top, a filter by kind, Recent brushes and the Starter brushes as stroke previews." caption="The brush library, with the current kit at the top." />

- **Filter** by kind (Dry, Wet, Lasso, Erasers) or search by name, group or the bundle it came from.
- **Recent** shows the last brushes you picked. Your own brushes are under **Mine**, the built-in
  ones under **Starter**, and imported bundles get their own groups.
- **Grid or list:** switch with the toolbar button.
- **New Brush** makes a Dry or Wet brush and opens it in the editor.
- **Import** brings in Krita bundles, presets and Photoshop brush tips. See
  [Import and export](/docs/import-and-export/#krita-brush-bundles).

Long-press a brush for more:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Add to Kit | Puts it in a slot of the current kit. |
| Assign to Finger | Makes it the brush your finger uses when you paint with Apple Pencil. |
| Add to Group | Files it in a group, or makes a new one. |
| Edit | Opens it in the brush editor. |
| Change Icon… | Draw an icon, or use a shape or a picture. |
| Duplicate | Makes a copy to change. |
| Hide from Library | Keeps it out of sight without deleting it. |
| Delete… | Removes it. Built-in brushes can be restored from **Settings › Resources › Brushes**. |

## Kits and the kit ring

<SearchTerms terms="kit, kit ring, favourite brushes, quick brushes, double tap, radial menu, brush wheel" />

A kit is eight brushes you keep close. They sit in a ring around your Pencil tip, at fixed compass
points, so your hand learns where each one is.

<Screenshot src={BrushesKitRing} alt="The kit ring open over a dark painting: eight brushes around a colour wheel, with value check, eyedropper and mirror below." caption="The kit ring: eight brushes, a colour wheel and three buttons." />

**Open the kit ring** by double-tapping Apple Pencil, or tap the ring button at the bottom of the
tool rail. The ring opens where the Pencil is. In it:

- **Eight brushes** around the edge. Tap one to paint with it.
- **A colour wheel** in the middle. Drag to change colour.
- **Three buttons** below: value check, eyedropper and mirror.

Touch anywhere outside the ring to close it.

**Make your own kits** at the top of the brush library. The kit menu switches between your saved
kits, **Recent Brushes** (the last eight you picked) or a **Group** (its first eight). **New Kit**
starts an empty one: drag brushes onto its slots, or long-press a brush and choose **Add to Kit**.

The same kit sits on the [scratchpad's](/docs/colour/#the-scratchpad) bar.

## The brush editor

<SearchTerms terms="brush settings, edit brush, customise, tip, spacing, pressure curve, tilt, sensors, dynamics, texture" />

Tap the dial on the tool rail to edit the current brush. Changes apply as you make them, so you can
paint with them straight away, and try them on the scribble pad at the top.

<Screenshot src={BrushesEditor} alt="The brush editor showing the Brush Tip page: tip shapes, diameter, angle and fade, with the option pages down the left." caption="The brush editor, on the Brush Tip page." />

Your changes are kept as a working copy until you decide:

- **Overwrite** saves them into the brush.
- **Save as New** makes a new brush and leaves the original as it was.
- **Revert** throws them away.

A dot in the library marks a brush with unsaved changes.

At the top: the brush's name and icon, its kind (**Dry**, **Wet** or **Lasso**), and quick
**Size**, **Opacity** and **Flow** sliders. Down the side, the brush's pages:

- **Tip:** the brush tip (round, soft, flat, chisel, a bitmap of your own…), spacing and shape.
- **Dynamics:** opacity, flow, size, rotation, softness and scatter.
- **Colour:** hue, saturation and value changes, and colour jitter per dab or per stroke.
- **Texture** and **Tooth:** a texture in the stroke, and how much the paper's grain catches light
  strokes.
- **Smudge** (Wet brushes): how far paint is dragged, how much colour the brush adds, and paint
  thickness.
- **Fill** (Lasso brushes): flat or gradient, and edge softness.

Most pages can be driven by the Pencil through **sensors**: pressure, tilt, its direction and
angle, barrel roll, speed, drawing angle, and fade, distance and time along the stroke. Tick a
sensor and shape its curve. Drag a point to move it, touch the graph to add one, and double-tap a
point to remove it.
