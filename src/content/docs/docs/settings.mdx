## General

<SearchTerms terms="artist name, resources, manage brushes, restore built-in, Easy Mode" />

- **Artist Name:** used on PDF art books and brush bundles you export.
- **Resources:** import and manage brushes, brush tips, textures, paper textures, book covers and
  palettes. Hide what you don't use, delete what you've imported, and restore any built-ins you've
  removed.
- **Easy Mode:** adds a single undo step. See [No undo, and Easy Mode](/docs/getting-started/#no-undo-and-easy-mode).

## Appearance and layout

<SearchTerms terms="theme, dark mode, light mode, background colour, left handed, lefty" />

- **Theme:** System, Light or Dark.
- **Canvas background:** the colour around the page when it doesn't fill the screen.
- **Left-handed layout:** moves the tool rail and colour panel to the left edge and the scratchpad
  to the right, out from under your hand.

## Painting

<SearchTerms terms="paint with, finger, Pencil mode, one finger, smudge finger" />

- **Paint with:** Apple Pencil or Finger. Pleinbook switches to Apple Pencil the first time it sees
  one, unless you've chosen here.
- **One finger** (with Apple Pencil): the brush your finger uses, Finger Smudge to start with, or
  None. Assign another from the brush library with **Assign to Finger**.

## Apple Pencil

<SearchTerms terms="double tap, squeeze, Pencil Pro, Pencil gestures, shortcuts" />

- **Squeeze** (Apple Pencil Pro): **Pick a colour** by default.
- **Double tap:** **Kit ring** by default.

Either can be Pick a colour, Kit ring, Scratchpad (shows or hides it) or Nothing. If the iPad's own Apple Pencil settings give the
gesture to something else, Pleinbook leaves it alone.

## Pressure and touch

<SearchTerms terms="pressure, calibrate, calibration, pressure curve, heavy hand, light touch, finger pressure, palm" />

- **Pencil pressure:** calibrate how hard you need to press for full pressure, so you never have to
  push harder than is comfortable, and shape the pressure curve. Try it on the test pad.
- **Finger pressure:** calibrate a light touch and a firm press, for painting with your finger.
- **Palm rejection:** for painting with your finger, measure your resting hand so it's told apart
  from a finger.

## Page details

<SearchTerms terms="location, place recording, where painted" />

- **Record place:** notes where each page was first painted. See [Place recording](/docs/sketchbooks/#place-recording).

## Sync and support

<SearchTerms terms="iCloud status, sync status, restore purchases, tip jar, support Pleinbook" />

- **Sync:** iCloud sync's status, with Pleinbook Unlock. See [iCloud sync](/docs/pleinbook-unlock/#icloud-sync).
- **Support Pleinbook:** buy or restore Pleinbook Unlock, or leave a tip.

## Diagnostics

<SearchTerms terms="version, latency, engine version, bug report" />

- **Input latency (estimate)** and **Brush engine version**. The brush
  engine version is useful in a [bug report](/support/#reporting-a-bug).
- **Acknowledgements:** the open-source software Pleinbook uses.
