## What's included

<SearchTerms terms="paid, purchase, premium, pro, upgrade, price, in-app purchase, what do I get" />

Pleinbook is free to paint with, forever. Pleinbook Unlock is a one-off purchase per Apple Account
that adds:

- Replay video of any page, from blank paper to the finished painting
- Whole sketchbook as a PDF art book
- 8 premium sketchbook covers
- 6 premium paper textures
- iCloud sync of your sketchbooks, brushes and settings across your devices

Unlock features show a crown and "Pleinbook Unlock" until you have it. Tap one to see the unlock.

## Replay videos

<SearchTerms terms="replay, timelapse, time-lapse, video, process video, recording, playback, mp4" />

Every stroke you make is recorded, so any page can be replayed from blank paper to the finished
painting. Long-press a page and choose **Export replay video…**.

- **Size:** the screen's size, half the page or the full page.
- **Speed:** automatic (most replays come out at 10 to 50 seconds), one stroke per frame, or 30 or
  60 seconds.

The page is repainted stroke by stroke with the same brushes, then holds on the finished painting.
Only the page is shown, not the scratchpad or the controls. When it's ready, share it or save it to
Photos.

A replay repaints your strokes with today's brush engine. If the engine has changed since you painted
the page, the replay can come out slightly different from the painting. The saved page itself is
never changed.

## Sketchbook PDFs

<SearchTerms terms="PDF, art book, print, portfolio, book export, export sketchbook" />

Turn a whole sketchbook into a PDF art book: long-press it in the library, or open its menu in the
page grid, and choose **Export PDF…**.

- **Cover:** the sketchbook's cover with a title and your name, in the typeface and colour you
  choose.
- **Pages:** every page in order, at 300 dpi.
- **Under each page**, optionally: its name, the dates it was made and last changed, its stroke
  count, the time spent painting, where it was painted, and the brushes used.

## iCloud sync

<SearchTerms terms="sync, iCloud, multiple devices, backup, iPad and iPhone, cloud, conflict, storage full" />

With Pleinbook Unlock, your sketchbooks, scratchpads, palettes, brushes, kits and settings sync
between your devices through your own iCloud. There's nothing to turn on: sign in to iCloud and it
starts, and everything you made before unlocking uploads too. Check its progress in
**Settings › Sync**.

A page's painting downloads when you open it on another device.

Some settings stay on each device, such as Pencil calibration, left-handed layout and the canvas
background.

**When two devices change the same page** before syncing, Pleinbook keeps both. The other device's
version stays, and yours appears right after it with a **Conflict copy** badge. Keep the one you
want and delete the other.

**When iCloud is full**, your work stays safe on your device and uploads when there's space.

**If you sign out of iCloud**, or Pleinbook Unlock is refunded, sync stops and nothing on your
device changes.

## Buying and restoring

<SearchTerms terms="buy, restore purchases, new iPad, family sharing, tip, tip jar, refund" />

Buy Pleinbook Unlock from any unlock feature, or from **Settings › Support Pleinbook**. On another
device signed in to the same Apple Account, tap **Restore Purchases** there.

You can also leave a tip in **Settings › Support Pleinbook**. Tips are optional and unlock nothing
extra, but are deeply appreciated.
