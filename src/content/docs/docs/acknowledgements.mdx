## In the app

**ZIPFoundation**: Copyright © 2017–2024 Thomas Zoechling. MIT License.

**spectral.js**: Copyright © 2025 Ronald van Wijnen. MIT License. Pleinbook's mixing weights each
paint by its luminance, an idea taken from spectral.js, and its tests compare against a
reimplementation of spectral.js's mix.

Pigment mixing uses Pleinbook's own Kubelka–Munk implementation, with the CIE 1931 observer
approximated by the multi-lobe Gaussian fit of Wyman, Sloan and Shirley (JCGT, 2013). Spectral basis
curves are computed with Scott Allen Burns' least hyperbolic tangent slope squared (LHTSS) method
(Color Research & Application, 2020). Mixes of two colourful paints are judged in Björn Ottosson's
OKLab colour space (2020).

Pleinbook imports Krita brush bundles and .kra files. Krita is a trademark of the Krita Foundation;
Pleinbook is not affiliated with Krita. Pleinbook contains no Krita code or resources.
