## Value check and mirror

<SearchTerms terms="greyscale, grayscale, black and white, values, tonal, flip, mirror, reverse, horizontal flip" />

**Value check** shows the page in greys, so you can judge light and dark without colour getting in
the way. Tap it in the top bar or on the kit ring, and tap again to go back. It only changes what you
see, never the page.

<Screenshot src={SeeingValueCheck} alt="The painted page shown in greys with value check on." caption="Value check on." />

**Mirror** flips the page left-to-right based on how you're viewing it, to show it with fresh eyes.
It's a real flip of the painting, so tap it again to flip back. You can paint while it's flipped.

## Drawing guides

<SearchTerms terms="guides, grid, perspective, vanishing point, horizon, rule of thirds, golden spiral, golden ratio, composition" />

Guides help you place things: a line, a grid, a perspective grid with one, two or three vanishing
points, or a golden spiral. They float over the page: they're never baked into the painting and never
snap your strokes, and you can show or hide them at any time.

1. Tap **Guides** in the top bar, then **+** and choose a guide.
2. Its settings open. While they're open, touches on the page move the guide instead of painting:
   drag to move it, and use two fingers to turn or resize it.
3. Set its colour and opacity, and kind-specific options such as the number of grid squares or
   perspective lines.

Each page keeps its own guides. Hide them all with the eye, or delete them with the bin.

<Screenshot src={SeeingGuides} alt="A one-point perspective guide over the page, with its settings card open." caption="A one-point perspective guide and its settings." />

## Adjustments

<SearchTerms terms="adjust, levels, curves, hue saturation, HSL, gradient map, colour correction, brightness, contrast" />

Tap **Adjustments** in the top bar to change the whole page: **Levels**, **HSL** (hue, saturation
and luminosity, all at once or by colour range), **Curves** or **Gradient Map**.

<Screenshot src={SeeingAdjustmentsLevels} alt="The Adjust page panel on Levels, with the page's histogram and input and output controls." caption="Adjustments, on Levels." />

The page updates as you adjust. Hold the eye button to compare with the original, then **Apply**, or
**Cancel** to leave the page as it was. Adjustments are applied straight to the page rather than as a
separate layer, changing the paint and the paper together. They never touch the scratchpad.

Without Easy Mode there's no undo after **Apply**.
