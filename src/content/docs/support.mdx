## Quick fixes

<QuickFixes>

<QuickFix q="The Pencil needs pressing too hard, or too lightly">

Open **Settings › Pencil Pressure** and calibrate. It sets how hard you need to press for full
pressure, so you never have to push harder than is comfortable. You can shape the pressure curve
there too.

</QuickFix>

<QuickFix q="Double-tap or squeeze isn't doing what I expect">

By default, double-tapping Apple Pencil opens the kit ring, and squeezing Apple Pencil Pro picks up
the colour under the tip. You can set each one to **Kit ring**, **Pick a colour** or **Nothing** in
**Settings › Apple Pencil**.

</QuickFix>

<QuickFix q="I don't have an Apple Pencil">

In **Settings › Painting**, set **Paint with** to **Finger**. Pressure then comes from how much of your
finger touches the glass, which you can calibrate in **Settings › Finger Pressure**.

</QuickFix>

<QuickFix q="I made a mark I didn't mean to">

We believe in a sketchbook being raw and authentic, so undo is off by default, even if that means
embracing mistakes and accidents. If you'd like a safety net while you learn, turn on **Easy Mode** in
Settings for a single undo step.

Once you're done painting, remember to lock the page (the padlock in the top bar) so you don't accidentally smudge or paint on it
later.

</QuickFix>

<QuickFix q="Pleinbook Unlock isn't showing on my other device">

Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought Pleinbook Unlock with, then tap
**Restore Purchases** in Settings. Pleinbook Unlock belongs to your Apple Account, so it works on all your
devices.

</QuickFix>

<QuickFix q="iCloud says storage is full">

Your work is safe on your device, and it uploads when there's space. Free up iCloud storage, or add
more in your device's Settings, and Pleinbook catches up on its own.

</QuickFix>

</QuickFixes>

## Reporting a bug

Email us and include:

- what you were doing, and what went wrong
- a screenshot or screen recording, if you can
- your device model and its iPadOS or iOS version (the device's **Settings › General › About**)
- your Apple Pencil model
- the brush engine version (Pleinbook **Settings › Diagnostics**)
