_Last updated 11 November 2026_

Pleinbook is built to stay out of your business. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and
no tracking, and we never see your paintings. This page explains the few places where information
does move, and who handles it.

## In short

- Your paintings, scratchpads, brushes and settings stay on your device, and in your own iCloud if
  you sync.
- We don't collect any data in the app. On the App Store, Pleinbook is labelled **Data Not
  Collected**.
- If you email us, we use what you send only to respond.
- This website has no analytics or tracking. The only cookie is a security check from our host,
  Cloudflare.

## Your paintings and settings

Everything you make in Pleinbook, from sketchbooks and pages to scratchpads, brushes, kits and
settings, is stored on your device. We don't run any servers for the app, and nothing you make is
ever sent to us.

**iCloud sync.** With Pleinbook Unlock, your work can sync between your devices through iCloud. It
goes to your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account. Apple stores it, and we can't see
it or get to it. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers iCloud.

**Photos.** When you import a photo, Pleinbook reads only the photos you pick, and only on your
device. Nothing is uploaded. When you save a page or a replay video to your photo library, your
device asks for permission first.

**Place recording.** Place recording is off unless you turn it on, in **Settings › Page Details**.
When it's on, Pleinbook notes where each page was first painted and stores that with the page. To
turn the location into a place name, it asks Apple's location services, so Apple receives the
location for that lookup. We never receive your location. You can turn place recording off at any
time.

## Purchases

Pleinbook Unlock and tips are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never
see your payment details or your name. Whether you own Pleinbook Unlock is checked on your device,
with Apple.

## Crash reports and app statistics

Pleinbook has no crash reporting or analytics of its own. If you've chosen to share analytics with
app developers (on your device, in **Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements**),
Apple may pass us anonymised crash reports and statistics, such as how many people open the app.
These don't identify you, and you can turn sharing off at any time.

## Getting in touch

If you email [support@pleinbook.com](mailto:support@pleinbook.com), we receive your email address
and whatever you include, such as screenshots or details about your device. We use these only to
respond to you, which is our legitimate interest in answering your message. We keep them only as long as
we need to sort out your question, and delete them within two years. We never add you to a mailing
list.

Our Discord server runs on Discord, so [Discord's privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy) covers
anything you post there.

## This website

pleinbook.com is a set of static pages hosted by Cloudflare. It runs no analytics or tracking, sets
no cookies of its own, and loads nothing from other companies. Even the fonts are served from this
site.

Our host, Cloudflare, may check that you're a person rather than an automated bot, and remember the
result in a security cookie (`cf_clearance`). It isn't used for tracking or
advertising, and it expires on its own. See Cloudflare's
[privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and
[cookie policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/cookie-policy/).

## Minors

Pleinbook doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including minors.
