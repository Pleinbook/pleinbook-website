### New

- Paint that mixes like pigment: blue and yellow make green, on the page, in smudges and on the
  scratchpad.
- Dry, Wet and Lasso brushes, each with a full editor: tip, spacing, scatter, texture, and curves for
  pressure, tilt and speed.
- Custom kits of up to eight brushes around the Pencil tip, opened with a double-tap.
- A scratchpad for every page, kept with the painting.
- Sketchbooks with their own size and paper, plus page lock to keep finished paintings safe.
- Import Krita brush bundles, Photoshop brush tips, photos and .kra files.
- Export pages as PNG or as a Krita file with separate paper and paint layers.
- Pleinbook Unlock: replay videos, whole sketchbooks as PDF art books, premium covers and papers, and
  iCloud sync across your devices.
